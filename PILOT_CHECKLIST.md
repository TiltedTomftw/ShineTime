# Shinetime Pilot Checklist (2-Week Plan)

Goal: Be ready to show the app publicly in 2 weeks.

---

## Daily Use (Week 1-2)

- Detailers log every job (start/pause/resume/complete)
- Add at least one note and one photo per job
- Sales checks status via the sales page for live vehicles
- Record 1-2 quick feedback items per day:
  - What is confusing?
  - What is slow?
  - What is missing?

---

## End-of-Week Wrap-Up (End of Each Week)

- Review dashboard stats and photo storage size
- List top 3 friction points
- Pick 1-2 small fixes for the next week

---

## Launch Readiness (End of Week 2)

- Confirm core flow works end-to-end on 2+ devices
- Ensure sales view returns accurate results by stock number
- Verify OCR scan works in typical lighting
- Confirm notes and photos render on dashboard
- Verify no crashes during a normal shift
- Decide if any "must-fix" issues remain before public demo
